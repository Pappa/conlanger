## Question

How do **optional stage outputs**, **nested context/I/O sets**, and **cartesian IO optionals** interact with fidelity and compile?

## Answer

Support **optional outputs** (alt idx notation) and **parse-time flattening** of nested sets where attested meaning is preserved. Cartesian IO optionals and Indo-Aryan optional-stage fixes are allowed when inventory proves mechanical recovery.

When attested forms cannot compile without distortion, use **human-review hold-outs** (`status`, `comment`) rather than silent rewrite.
